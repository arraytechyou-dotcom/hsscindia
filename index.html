Hydrocarbon Sector Skill Council (HSSC)

Hydrocarbon Sector Skill Council (HSSC), is a sector skill council for oil & gas sector, has been set up under the aegis of Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas (MoP&NG), and is a recognized Awarding Body with National Council of Vocational Education & Training (NCVET) under Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship (MSDE). The main objective of HSSC is to execute skill development activities in hydrocarbon Sector and to meet the entire value chainâ€™s requirement of appropriately trained manpower on a sustained and evolving basis. Vision

We are a world class skill council meeting diverse skill requirements of the Hydrocarbon Industry and enhancing employability and opportunities, locally and globally.

Mission

  • ✓ Creating a pool of skilled manpower for the hydrocarbon sector
  • ✓ Benchmarking new skills and up-skilling National Occupational Standards (NOS)
  • ✓ Promoting inclusive growth through economic and social equity
  • ✓ Reaping the â€˜Demographic Dividendâ€™
  • ✓ Improving productivity and sector competitiveness

Objective

The objective of HSSC is to execute skill development activities within the hydrocarbon sector in India. It aims to meet the comprehensive training needs across the entire value chain, ensuring a sustainable supply of appropriately skilled manpower. This involves identifying sector-specific skill requirements, developing targeted skill development plans, and establishing competency standards and qualifications.
